What happens in the room

Not a lecture about AI.
A moment with it.

A keynote by Lukas Wagner is built in five acts, like a good evening at the theatre.

  1. Act 01

    Arrival

    People arrive with questions, half-knowledge and healthy scepticism. Exactly right.

  2. Act 02

    Understanding

    What is relevant, what stays hype. Plain language instead of jargon.

  3. Act 03

    Experience

    The audience gives input, the AI delivers live. Amazement, laughter, debate.

  4. Act 04

    Perspective

    Opportunities and limits, without alarmism and without glorification.

  5. Act 05

    Action

    The moment turns into direction: concrete next steps.

What stays

People arrive with questions.
They leave with a direction.

A shared language for AI

Teams and leadership talk about the same thing afterwards, not about ten different buzzwords.

Realistic perspective

What AI can really do today, what it cannot, and what that means for your own work.

Concrete next steps

Ideas you can try on the next working day. Without a new shopping list.

No transformation overnight. But a shared starting point that holds.
